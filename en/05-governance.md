# 🏛️ Governance & Citizen Assemblies
{: .no_toc }

The full pipeline from citizen proposals to actual policy through deliberation. Beyond simple proposal-collection windows, Parti has built a multi-layered citizen-collaboration platform model — citizen proposal → deliberation → citizen assembly·voting → policy. The model is published as the DemosX guide, enabling anyone to build their own public square, and has reached the stage where AI-assisted large-scale opinion analysis allows citizens themselves to identify agendas.

## Platform Features
{: .text-delta }

Core features DemosX provides for citizen-collaboration platforms.

### 1. Citizen Proposal System

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A system where any citizen can submit a policy proposal based on problems observed in everyday life and their neighborhoods. Supports anonymous or identified submission, file attachments, and automatic linking of similar proposals.

[DemosX Citizen Collaboration Platform](https://demosx.org){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 2. Deliberation Process

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

A structured process where proposals are deliberated through citizen discussion, expert input, and institutional responses — not decided by simple majority vote. The multi-layered flow — "topic curation → preliminary review → preliminary discussion → expert forum → open dialogue forum → post-discussion → citizen panel evaluation → maturation forum → policy" — is the core of DemosX, refined through a decade of Parti's operational experience.

### 3. AI-Based Large-Scale Opinion Analysis

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

Automatic classification, summarization, and category-pattern extraction by AI from thousands to tens of thousands of citizen opinions. Goes beyond simple majority vote and enables citizens themselves to identify agendas — a new stage of governance. AI analysis results are then verified through expert review, citizen panel deliberation, and voting before becoming confirmed agendas that flow into substantive social dialogue.

### 4. Citizen Assemblies & Citizen Panels

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

Tools for running citizen representatives — selected by lottery or recruitment — through in-depth policy deliberation. Supports random sortition, panel workshops, drafting consensus statements, and announcing results.

### 5. Citizen Voting & Policy Decision

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A system for citizens to directly decide on deliberated proposals through voting. Combined with Parti Townhall's large-scale voting capacity, it is used for participatory budgeting, citizen general assemblies, and policy decisions.

[Parti Townhall](https://townhall.kr){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 6. Policy-Implementation Tracking

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A feature allowing citizens to follow the full journey from proposal to policy. Review·examination·decision·implementation stages are visually displayed, with progress and institutional responses transparently shown.

### 7. Operations Guide & Checklist System

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

An operations guide and checklist that institutions adopting a citizen-collaboration platform can follow step by step. A distinctive Parti asset consolidating 200+ public forums of operational experience. Covers everything from stage-by-stage goal-setting to operational principles and practical checklists.

[DemosX Guide](https://demosx.org){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 8. An Open Model and Operating Guide

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

The DemosX model was first released in 2017, as open source and an open guide, through Parti's collaboration with the Seoul Metropolitan Government. Openness alone gave no way to share maintenance costs, and small organizations that wanted to adopt it had no technical staff to build it themselves, so Parti later moved it to a service. What is open today is the DemosX guide, drawn from 200+ public forums; Mixon is the solution for building a platform along that guide. Institutions don't need to start from scratch — they can adopt a validated model and adapt it to their own context. The conviction that the citizens' square belongs to citizens, carried forward by opening the model and the know-how.

### 9. Citizen Policy Lab

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A program that takes an agenda — proposed by citizens, or raised by a region or institution — and runs it as a small-scale experiment before it becomes policy, to test whether it actually works and whether citizens accept it. It continues and expands what Parti has run under the name "Testing Ground." The process runs in five stages: selecting the agenda, designing the experiment, running it (typically three to six months), analyzing results, and connecting the findings to public deliberation and policy. The craft lies in framing an experiment question concrete enough for the answer to be visible — for example, "If we place a doll on the priority seat, will only pregnant passengers sit there?" This is the missing middle step where a citizen proposal, instead of dying in the binary of accepted or rejected, grows through experiment and deliberation. Through DemosX, Parti has supported 107 citizen experiment teams cumulatively as of 2025, and results feed into Galaxy Vote, Star Talk, and Chorus as the starting point for deliberation. (Paired with "Civic Tech Lab" in Category 6, Civic Technology.)

[Citizen Policy Lab Toolkit](https://campaigns.do/toolkits/testing-ground){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

## Governance Implementations
{: .text-delta }

Citizen-collaboration platforms operated on DemosX in Korea.

### 10. Democracy Seoul / Sangsangdaero Seoul

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

The Seoul Metropolitan Government's citizen-collaboration platform. Parti led the planning and operations from 2017–2019, building it into a model where Seoul's 10 million citizens could directly propose and decide on policy. Winner of the 2019 iF Design Award (Service Design). Renamed "Sangsangdaero Seoul" in December 2022 and continues operating.

[Sangsangdaero Seoul](https://idea.seoul.go.kr){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 11. Gwanghwamun 1st Avenue / Policy Dialogue Forum

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

The Ministry of the Interior and Safety's whole-of-government citizen-participation platform. Parti has operated the Open Dialogue Forum since 2021, which evolved into the Policy Dialogue Forum — a cross-government public square where citizens and government deliberate together on rapidly changing social issues.

### 12. National Cohesion Platform

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

The Presidential Committee for National Cohesion's citizen-participation platform. Through "Field-Based National Dialogue," 4,215+ citizen proposals are gathered and organized into six domains (politics·ideology, polarization, regional, generational, gender, social vulnerability). AI analysis is used to identify agendas, which are then verified through expert review, citizen panel deliberation, and voting before flowing into substantive social dialogue. The most advanced implementation of Parti's multi-layered citizen-collaboration platform model.

[National Cohesion Platform](https://cohesion.or.kr){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 13. Ten Million Connections

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

An online public square where citizens dream together about the world to build after President Yoon's removal. Over approximately three months, 788 citizen opinions were collected and classified into nine domains using AI-based analysis. The results flow into policy development by 11 subcommittees of the Social Reform Special Committee, and inform the platforms and policies of political parties. A case where Parti's AI-based large-scale opinion analysis was applied to civil-society agenda-finding.

[Ten Million Connections Report](https://talk.bisang1203.net/pages/10million-connections-report2){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 14. Climate Citizens' Assembly

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A citizens' assembly and public deliberation project for amending the Carbon Neutrality Act and addressing the climate crisis. Citizens directly propose agendas and engage in dialogue, ensuring citizen voices are reflected in legislation and policy decisions.

[Climate Citizens' Assembly](https://talks.campaigns.do/goals/climate-public-deliberation){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 15. AI Democracy Citizen Dialogue

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A public forum where citizens themselves ask and talk through the question of democracy in the AI era. Through the lecture series "Asking Democracy Again in the AI Era" and the gathering of citizen questions, citizens deliberate together on what AI should mean for them. (Also covered in Category 11, AI & Future Technology.)

[AI Democracy Citizen Questions](https://talks.campaigns.do/goals/aidemocracy){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 16. AI Democracy Citizen Lab

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A space where citizens and workers work hands-on with AI to generate ideas. Through the "AI Ideathon for Workers and Workplaces," citizens experiment directly with how they might use and reshape AI as it enters workplaces and daily life. A full citizens' assembly in the proper sense is treated as a future direction in Category 11, AI & Future Technology.

### 17. Korea Talks (Hankyoreh × Parti)

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

A social-conflict-resolution dialogue program jointly operated by Hankyoreh (Korea's major progressive newspaper) and Parti. Uses Parti's Galaxy Vote and Star Talk methodology to match citizens with the most differing views and conduct in-depth one-on-one dialogue. Opinion changes before and after dialogue are tracked as data.

[Korea Talks](https://talks.campaigns.do/goals/parti-talks){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 18. Seoul Education Communication Square

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

The Seoul Office of Education's citizen-collaboration platform. An open participation space where students, parents, teachers, and citizens can share opinions and propose ideas on Seoul's education policy.

### 19. Regional Citizen-Collaboration Platforms

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

Platforms operated in partnership with local governments and regional communities. Examples include "I'm In Busan" (social problem-solving platform), Eunpyeong District's "Forest of Participation" (online policy platform), Korea YWCA's "Medical Public Discourse Platform," Gyeonggi Province's village-policy platform, and DemosX-based platforms in Miryang, Jeonju, Chuncheon, Wanju, and other regions.

[DemosX Project Portfolio](https://demosx.org/pages/demosx-works){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

## Future Directions
{: .text-delta }

The next leap for citizen governance.

### 20. Advanced AI Opinion Classification & Synthesis

⬜ Future Direction
{: .label }

Further development of current AI analysis tools — extending beyond agenda-finding to automatic identification and visualization of points of citizen agreement and divergence. Letting citizens see immediately where their own opinions sit in the larger conversation.

### 21. Blockchain-Verified Citizen Decision-Making

⬜ Future Direction
{: .label }

A feature that verifies and records large-scale citizen decision outcomes on blockchain for trust. Combined with identity verification, residence confirmation, and tamper-resistance, ensures that citizen-decided policies are reliably protected.
