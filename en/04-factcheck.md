# ✅ Fact-Checking
{: .no_toc }

Citizen fact-checking in the age of disinformation. Parti built Korea's first crowdsourced fact-checking platform with citizen participation and operates a community where citizens themselves become fact-checkers, building truth together. Beyond after-the-fact verification, Parti is developing proactive responses that track the circulation paths of disinformation.

## Platform Features
{: .text-delta }

Tools on Campaigns.do for citizens to write and consume fact-checks.

### 1. Fact-Check Writing Format

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A dedicated writing format for fact-checks on Campaigns.do. Writers can more easily compose fact-check articles, structuring verdicts and evidence with clarity.

[Fact-Check on Campaigns.do](https://campaigns.do/discussions?template=fact_check){: .btn .btn-outline .fs-3 }

### 2. Fact-Check Reader Interface

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A display format designed for readers to grasp fact-check content at a glance. Verdicts (True / False / Pending) are immediately visible with evidence and sources alongside.

### 3. Fact-Check Search & Archive

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

A searchable archive of past fact-checks. Citizens can revisit previously verified topics.

## Community & Methodology
{: .text-delta }

The community where citizens themselves work as fact-checkers, along with the education and operations behind it.

### 4. K.F.C. Citizen Fact-Checkers Community

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

Korean Fact-Checkers Community. A community Parti operates where citizens themselves work as fact-checkers — learning, verifying, and growing together. A pioneering case in Korea that moved beyond expert-institution-centered fact-checking toward a citizen-participation model.

### 5. Citizen Fact-Check Education & Training

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

Education and training programs Parti runs to help citizens grow into fact-checkers. Systematic learning covers information verification methods, source-checking techniques, and fact-check writing.

### 6. FactCheck.net (Former Initiative)

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }
⭐ Parti Original
{: .label .label-purple }

The first crowdsourced fact-checking platform in Korea, operated 2021–2023 with Parti as co-chair. Introduced a collaborative model where journalists, experts, and citizens fact-check together, making decisive contributions to building Korea's fact-checking ecosystem.

## Connection & International
{: .text-delta }

Collaboration with domestic and international fact-checking ecosystems.

### 7. IFCN Global Fact-Checking Fund (Build Support)

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

In 2024, Parti was selected for the Global Fact-Checking Fund's "Build Support" program by the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN). A case of international recognition for Korea's citizen fact-checking model.

### 8. Media & Journalism Collaboration

✅ Implemented
{: .label .label-green }

Joint fact-checking projects with newsrooms and media organizations. A collaboration model combining citizens' verification capacity with journalism's investigative power.

## Future Directions
{: .text-delta }

The next challenges in the age of disinformation.

### 9. Disinformation Pathway Tracking & Proactive Alert

⬜ Future Direction
{: .label }

A system that tracks how disinformation is created, what paths it travels along, and how it mutates across communities. A proactive response model that goes beyond after-the-fact verification to identify disinformation propagation paths in advance and alert citizens. 

### 10. AI-Assisted Fact-Checking

⬜ Future Direction
{: .label }

AI tools that assist fact-checkers in their verification work. AI automates evidence search, source verification, and similar-case recommendations, reducing the workload of citizen fact-checkers. The direction is AI assisting citizens' verdicts, not AI making verdicts. (Also covered in Category 11, AI & Future Technology.)

### 11. Fact-Check Impact Tracking

⬜ Future Direction
{: .label }

Automatic tracking and visualization of fact-check impact — how widely verified information spreads, whether the spread of original disinformation declines — to make fact-checking effects visible.
